Ledger Groups And Ledgers

Groups organise the ledgers. Ledgers are the accounts that transactions are posted to. Only the Chief Accountant can change either.

Default groups cannot be deleted. A sub-group always has the same nature as its group. A custom group can be deleted only when it is inactive and holds no ledgers or sub-groups.

Ledger Group

A heading that ledgers sit under.

The nature (Assets, Liabilities, Income or Expenses) follows the group you choose.

A group with active ledgers or active sub-groups cannot be made inactive.

Default Group

This group came with the system. Its place and nature are fixed because reports depend on them.

Bank Accounts has active ledgers, so it cannot be made inactive.

Delete Group?

Duties And Taxes - GST will be deleted permanently. This cannot be undone. The audit log keeps a record of it.

Ledger

An account that transactions are posted to.

Prefix style: a plain hyphen with a space on each side, Title Case.

Shown only for ledgers under Assets and Liabilities groups. Leave empty for no opening balance.

Pick the date this balance is as on. Required when there is an amount.
