Menu behaviour

Menu groups open like an accordion: opening one closes the others. Items sit under their group as a tree.

On a large screen, use the panel button in the top bar to collapse the menu to icons and bring it back. On a phone the menu is a drawer opened with the three-line button.

Clicking a group icon while the menu is collapsed opens the menu on that group.
