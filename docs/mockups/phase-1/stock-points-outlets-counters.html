Stock Points, Outlets And Counters

Stock points hold the stock. Outlets sell, at their own rates. Counters are the billing points of an outlet.

Stock moves between stock points. It never moves between outlets.

How They Connect
  • Liquor Store stock point
    • No outlet sells from it. Stock is only transferred out.
  • Spadikam stock point
    • Spadikam outlet · 2 counters
  • Dhruvam stock point
    • Dhruvam outlet · 1 counter
    • No 20 outlet · no counters
  • No Stock Point
    • Malarvadi outlet
    • Front Office outlet
    • Touchings outlet

Stock Point

A place where stock is kept. Stock points cannot be deleted. Deactivate one that closes.

Letters and numbers only.

Cannot Deactivate Dhruvam

Two active outlets still sell from this stock point: Dhruvam and No 20. Move them to another stock point, or to none, first. Stock must also be zero or transferred out.

Outlet

A place that sells. Outlets cannot be deleted. Deactivate an outlet that closes.

Only one. Choose where this outlet's stock comes from. Several outlets may share a stock point and still keep their own selling rates. Leave as No Stock Point for an outlet that sells nothing from stock.

Counter

A billing point inside an outlet.
