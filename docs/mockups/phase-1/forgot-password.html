Forgot Password?

The System Admin can reset it for you.

Password reset emails are not used. Your sign in address is not a real mailbox.
  1. Tell the System Admin that you forgot your password. Cashiers: your PIN.
  2. They give you a temporary password or PIN.
  3. Sign in with it, then choose a new one.
