Liquor Items

The liquor inventory master. Every BevCo item is kept here. Active items are the ones you stock now; Inactive ones are on the BevCo list and can be bought later.

Rates and costs shown are the list rates in force on the chosen date, worked out from the rate history. Items are never deleted, only made Inactive.

Average cost and stock on hand are added to this list in Phase 4, when purchases and stock movements are built. Average cost is not a master-data value: it is worked out from the actual purchases.

Categories and types are kept in tables, not fixed in the code. A category that has items is made Inactive, never deleted.
